Read before quoting a figure

  • Regional coverage is observed, not allocated. It is verified against national totals for 12 countries and effectively ends in 2015. The population denominator is a 2023 snapshot, so regional rates are indicative rather than same-year.
  • Six member states publish no nurse data at all (BG, CY, LU, PL, PT, SE). Any EU-wide nurse total that includes them understates coverage by about a fifth of the member states.
  • Missing is not zero. Germany reports hospital beds through 2019 and nothing after. Countries that did not report are shown as "not reported", never as a measurement of zero.
  • Every tree-based model lost to the naive baseline on this data, because short near-linear series cannot be extrapolated beyond a training range. Reporting a gradient-boosted forecast here would look more sophisticated and be worse.
  • Hospital risk score weights are stated assumptions, not fitted parameters, and need domain review before informing a funding decision.
